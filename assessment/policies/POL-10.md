# POL-10 — Risk actions, dependencies, and exceptions

Version: 1.0

Status: authored exercise criterion

Risk and dependency findings use the project risk matrix for prioritization and target dates. A
policy exception requires a rationale, named owner role, review date, and explicit residual-risk
statement; an unavailable or stale scanner feed is recorded as a limitation rather than a clean scan.

This is a project-authored lab criterion, not a regulatory quotation.
